# Objections

The ones we hear more than twice, and the answer that has worked. Add to this file from call notes; include
the deal name in the PR so we can check what happened next.

## "Our CRM data is too messy for this to work"

Everyone's is. We start read-only from whatever is there and show the forecast next to their manual one. The
usual outcome is that the mess is visible for the first time, which becomes the RevOps initiative that pays
for us. Do not promise cleaning; promise visibility.

## "We already do this in a spreadsheet"

Yes, and it takes a day and a half each week (ask them to count). The spreadsheet also has no memory: it
cannot show what the forecast said four weeks ago. History is the thing they cannot build themselves.

## "The CRM vendor has a forecasting module"

It does. It reports what reps typed. We model what happens to deals like these, which is a different
question. Offer to run both for a quarter.

## "Reps will game it"

Reps game anything they can see. We do not show reps their own accuracy score; leaders see it. The deal-level
signals come from activity and stage history, not from what the rep says on Monday.

## "We are too small"

Sometimes true. Under 10 quota carriers, say so and leave the door open. Under Series B, say so and do not
leave the door open; see the ICP.

## "Security review will take months"

Read-only CRM scope, EU hosting, SOC 2 Type II since March 2026. Send the trust page before they ask.
Procurement is the long pole, not security.

## "Can you also enrich our accounts / find emails / dedupe the CRM?"

No. We are not a data vendor. Point them at the RevOps initiative in the plan, and note the request in the
CRM: it tells us how messy their data is, which predicts implementation time.
