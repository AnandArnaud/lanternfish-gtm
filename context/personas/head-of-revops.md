# Head of RevOps

Titles: Head of Revenue Operations, Director RevOps, VP Revenue Operations, Sales Operations Lead at
smaller companies. Reports to the head of GTM or the CFO. Exists from about Series B; before that a sales
leader does it badly on Fridays.

## What they own

The CRM and everything attached to it: stages, fields, routing, enrichment, territories, comp plans, the
forecast roll-up. They are the one person who knows why the data is wrong.

## Why they buy us

The weekly forecast takes them a day and a half to assemble by hand. They want the roll-up to be automatic
and defensible so the Monday meeting is about deals, not about whether the spreadsheet is right.

## What they fear

Another tool that writes to the CRM. They have been burned by integrations that overwrote fields. Ask about
the last tool they ripped out; the answer tells you how to run the pilot.

## How to sell to them

Pilot read-only first. Show the forecast matching their manual one within 5%, then show the three deals
where it does not and why. Give them the mapping doc before they ask.

## Signals that they are in buying mode

Hiring a RevOps analyst, a CRM clean-up project, a new VP Sales who wants their own forecast, a board
request for forecast accuracy history.
