# Head of Growth

Titles: Head of Growth, VP Growth, Director of Growth, sometimes VP Marketing with a pipeline number.
Common at product-led and hybrid companies between Series B and D.

## What they own

Pipeline creation, conversion from signup or MQL to opportunity, often the SDR team. Their number is sourced
pipeline, not closed revenue, which is why they care about us: when sales misses, growth gets blamed for
pipeline quality.

## Why they buy us

They want proof that the pipeline they sourced was good and that the miss happened downstream. They also
want to see which segments actually close, because their spend follows it.

## What they already have

A warehouse, a BI tool, a product analytics tool, and a spreadsheet that ties them together on Thursday
night. They are technical enough to pull their own data and allergic to tools that cannot be queried.

## How to sell to them

Show the segment-level close rates on their own data in the first call. Talk about attribution of the
miss, not forecasting. They will introduce us to the head of GTM once they trust the numbers.

## Watch out

They can be a champion with no budget. Confirm who signs by the second call.
