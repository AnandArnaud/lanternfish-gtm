# Head of GTM

Titles we see: Head of GTM, VP Go-to-Market, Chief Revenue Officer at smaller companies, occasionally
Chief Commercial Officer. Reports to the CEO. Owns sales, often marketing and CS too.

## What their week looks like

Monday forecast call with the CEO. Board deck every quarter, built the weekend before. Rep one-on-ones where
the real pipeline status comes out. They spend more time reconciling numbers than coaching, and they know
it.

## What they are measured on

Net new ARR against plan. Forecast accuracy, informally: the CEO remembers every miss.

## Why they buy us

They got burned. A quarter closed 30% under a number they had committed to the board three weeks earlier.
They want to know, by week two of the quarter, whether the number is real.

## What they say in discovery

"My reps are optimists." "I know the pipeline is inflated, I just do not know by how much." "The CRM is
right about 60% of the time."

## Who else is in the room

The CEO (economic buyer above Series B), RevOps (implementation and the person who actually uses the
product daily), a finance partner who wants the number in their model.

## What loses the deal

Making them feel audited. We are on their side; the forecast is the enemy, not the leader.
