# Proof

Customer stories we are allowed to use. Fictional names are not allowed here; every line below has a
named contact who agreed in writing. If a customer churns, move the story to the bottom under "retired".

## Tidewater Labs (Series C, developer tools, 410 employees, Berlin)

Problem: three quarters in a row closed 20 to 35% under the commit. Board asked for a forecast-accuracy
history that did not exist.
What we did: connected read-only in week one, back-filled eight quarters from CRM history, ran our model
next to their roll-up for one quarter.
Result: Q2 2026 closed within 4% of the week-three forecast. The RevOps lead cut forecast prep from nine
hours a week to two. Quote (Head of RevOps): "The first thing it did was prove our spreadsheet was wrong by
a quarter of a million. That paid for it."

## Harbourline (Series B, HR tech, 160 employees, Lyon)

Problem: founder-led sales handing over to a first VP Sales who inherited a pipeline nobody trusted.
Result: 38% of the open pipeline was flagged as stale in the first week and closed out. The VP Sales
re-set the quarter target on real pipeline and hit it. Renewed at 2x seats in September.

## Orrin Systems (public, enterprise software, 2,300 employees, UK)

Problem: regional forecasts in four spreadsheets with four definitions of "commit".
Result: one definition, one roll-up, weekly. Forecast call shortened from 90 minutes to 35. Expansion to
the US region in Q3 2026. Reference available for public-company deals only.

## Numbers we can quote in aggregate

Median forecast-accuracy improvement at week three across customers with two or more quarters of data:
from 71% to 88%. Median RevOps prep time saved: 6.5 hours a week. Fourteen reference customers as of
October 2026.
