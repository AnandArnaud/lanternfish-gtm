# Outbound

Owner: Tom. Volume: 60 new accounts a week, 3 touches each over 12 working days. Tier B accounts from the
scored list; Tier A accounts only with Priya's sign-off because she works those herself.

## Who

A head of GTM, growth or RevOps at an ICP account that shows at least one signal from
[signals.md](../signals.md). No signal, no outbound; the list sits in nurture until something happens.

## Sequence

1. Day 1, email. One observation about their company (the signal), one sentence on what we do, one
   question. Under 80 words. No deck.
2. Day 3, LinkedIn connect with no note.
3. Day 6, email. The Tidewater number (forecast within 4% at week three) and an offer to run read-only
   on their CRM for a quarter.
4. Day 9, call. Leave a 20-second voicemail if no answer.
5. Day 12, break-up email. Ask who owns the forecast if not them.

## What we need from the data

A verified work email (we do not have one for anyone in `data/contacts.csv` today), the right title at the
right company, and the signal with its date. Tom spends roughly half his week assembling this by hand.
Fixing that is the point of initiatives 1 and 2.

## Numbers from Q3

1,180 accounts sequenced, 7.1% reply rate, 2.3% meeting rate, 27 meetings, 9 opportunities, 2 closed.
Reply rate on sequences with a dated signal was 11%; without, 3%. Stop sending without a signal.
