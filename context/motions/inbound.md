# Inbound

Owner: Tom for response, Priya for qualification. Inbound is small (about 40 leads a month) and mostly
right: 60% of inbound leads in Q3 were ICP, against 25% of the CRM as a whole.

## Sources

The website form, the ROI calculator, webinar registrations, review-site referrals, partner referrals from
two CRM consultancies, and word of mouth from RevOps communities (our best source and the one we cannot
measure).

## Response rule

Under one business hour, by a person, with one qualifying question. Not a calendar link. Calendar links
go to the 40% who answer the question.

## Qualification on the first call

Stage, what they sell, how many quota carriers, who owns the forecast today and how long it takes them.
Tier A or B with a named forecast owner goes to Priya within the day. Tier C gets the self-serve trial and
a nurture track.

## Known problem

Inbound leads land in the CRM without a domain half the time (the form asks for a work email, the CRM
stores the email, nobody fills the company domain). Matching them to existing accounts is manual, and it
is how most of the duplicates in `data/accounts.csv` got created.
